About Us | TicketLord

A Brief Background on Our Movement and Values

TicketLord isn't just about selling tickets. We make it easier for people to experience the moments that matter to them and creating long lasting memories to share with friends

How it Started

We're a small team that got tired of missing shows and overpaying for tickets from resellers. So we came up with something. TicketLord was made because we believe that getting tickets shouldn't be challenging or stressful. Everyone should have a chance to see the things they love.

What We believe

We believe that a live show is something everyone should experience at least once in their life. There's nothing like the energy of the crowd at a live event. Those are the memories people hold forever and we think getting there should be easy and stress free

What Matters to us

Fairness. You will never receive fake tickets at TicketLord. You don't have to worry about resellers selling multiple people the same ticket. We have the tickets you need for the events that you want to see.

People First

We put the customers first because without the customers, we wouldn't be here. That means we have helpful support and clear information to make the buying process as smooth as possible. We have 24/7 support teams to answer any questions you the customer may have.

Simplicity

We have tried to make everything as simple as possible at TicketLord, without compromising efficiency. Just find your choice of entertainment, buy your tickets and go.

Why We Do It

We know how annoying it is not being able to get tickets to our favourite events. TicketLord was made to try and get rid of this experience entirely because everyone should have a fair chance.

We're here for everyone from music enjoyers to theatre lovers and for anyone that just wants a fun night out with friends.

Thank you all for supporting us here at TicketLord, where entertainment begins.
